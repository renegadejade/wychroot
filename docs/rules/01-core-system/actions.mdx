# Actions

When you as an individual or the enclave as a collective do something during the game to interact with or influence the world in some way, it's referred to as an action. Usually, performing an action is as simple as stating your intention and narrating what happens. There are four general types of actions:

- Encounter Actions
- Downtime Actions
- Organizing Actions
- Direct Actions

## Action Rolls

When you attempt something _difficult_, _dangerous_, or _dramatic_, you make an _action roll_ to see how things go.

Actions rolls are used to determine if an action results in a `hit`, `snag`, or `error` and whether or not a MESHnet `glitch` occurs.

To make the roll, you build a dice pool, roll them, and interpret the results to see what happens.

### Building a Dice Pool

Dice pools are made up of between one and six `d6`, the number of which depending on the type of action and the ratings involved. They all follow the same basic pattern:

Function (`1d`-`3d`) + Assets (`+0d`-`+2d`) + Variables (`-1d`-`+1d`)

### Interpreting Result

To interpret the results of the action roll, you take the **single highest result** and compare it to the table below to see if you get a `hit`, `snag`, or `error`. Additionally, if you rolled **doubles**—two or more of _any result_—you also get a `glitch`.

| Rol Result | Result  | Effect           |
| ---------- | ------- | ---------------- |
| `1,2,3`    | `error` | Things go poorly |
| `4,5`      | `snag`  | Things go okay   |
| `6`        | `hit`   | Things go great  |

:::info Glitch
When you roll _doubles_—two or more of any result—you get a `glitch`, or narrative twist in the form of a MESHnet bug. This could be an immediate or future bonus, an opportunity, or a bargain.
:::

:::tip Action Roll Example
You roll `4d` and get `[6,4,2,2]`

You get a `hit`, because the `6` is the single highest result. You also get a `glitch`, because of the double `2` results.
:::

“I’m going to walk over and burst through that door,” or “the enclave gathers for movie night,” for example.
