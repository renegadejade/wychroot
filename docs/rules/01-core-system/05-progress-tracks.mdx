# Progress Tracks

A progress track is a series of connected circles (see examples at right) that are used to track progress towards something over time by filling them in, in order (a “mark”). They are a visual reminder of how close you are to an event, accomplishment, or danger.

Through out the rules, you will be instructed to **mark** and **clear** tracks. When you mark, fill in a number of segments as noted (“mark 2 spirit”). When told to clear a track, erase that many marked segments.
When you mark the last segment in a track, the event, accomplishment, or danger manifests.
