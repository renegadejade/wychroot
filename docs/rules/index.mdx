# Introduction

Let's discover _Wychroot_ in less than five minutes.

## Gritty Solarpunk Roleplaying

### Build Community

Wychroot is, at it's core, a game about building community in the face of an exestensial threat. You are part of an enclave--a small community of people that live near each other, share resources, and work together to defend themselves and the enclave from a powerful adversary.

### Make Cool Shit

Characters in _Wychroot_ are solarpunk maker-heroes, to contrast cyberpunk's hacker-heores (but don't worry there is plenty of hacking to be had!). There is a powerful and freeform crafting system to allow you to tinker with contracptions, experiment with concotions, and, yes: write your own custom hacking scripts.

### Fight Fascists from Mars

As you build community and create new gear, you will have to defend the enclave from the **Dominion of Mars**. Recently reappearing after a ~300+ year absence, the Dominon expected to find Earth void of human settlements--and ready for resource extration. Make them regret it.
