# Menagerie

### Symbiosis
Your menagerie helps you when you ___. Take `+E` when the menagerie helps you do it. 

### Sprite Intelligence Network

You can mark spirit to reveal a secret, vulnerability, weakness, solution, or opportunity, about the current situation. You and your crew each take `+1d` on your next action roll when acting on this information.

> _What did your sprites find out?_

## Mods

### The Ancient Oak
You have armor[1] against physical injury.  
### The Vicious Bloodroot
Take `+1d` when you `Exploit` with the intent of causing physical damage.

### Custom
Take a mod from another creation.
